# Camera move

Wrap the selection in a rig. The outer frame is the shot. The inner group holds the artwork and is the only layer that moves.

- Pan moves the inner group.
- Zoom scales it.
- Roll rotates it.

Leave the design frames at their original size. A grid tour travels across the cards, then returns to the full view.

Write the keyframe times and the easing next to the rig.
