# Usability guide

Read the flow. Place questions on the canvas next to the frame they belong to.

- Ground each task in the participant's goal.
- Ask open questions. A yes or no answer ends the turn.
- Keep the question count small on a simple frame and longer on a complex one.
- Leave the expected answer out of the question.

Add a short opening script and a closing that asks what they would do next. The guide should be readable by someone who did not design the flow.
